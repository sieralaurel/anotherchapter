01 / THE IDEA

Stories are better when they’re shared.

Another Chapter brings people together through books, thoughtful conversations, and the little connections that happen in between.

Created for people who enjoy reading and exchanging perspectives, our gatherings offer a relaxed, welcoming space where stories can spark meaningful conversations. Volume 01 is an intimate first chapter: just 10 guests, one shared table, and two hours set aside to connect.

03 / THE SETTING

One table. Many different points of view.

An intimate table setting, made for lingering.

Guests will gather around one shared table, styled with aesthetic decorations, carefully selected art books, and thoughtful details. The aim is a warm, inspiring atmosphere that feels personal rather than formal.
